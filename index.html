Help & FISD GPA Policy

Unweighted GPA (4.0 scale)

Traditional college-comparable GPA. All courses count equally.

  • 90–100 → 4.0
  • 80–89 → 3.0
  • 70–79 → 2.0
  • Below 70 → 0.0

Weighted GPA (FISD Rank GPA)

Used for class rank, top 10%, valedictorian/salutatorian, and honor graduates. Based on semester grades.

Quick Tips

  • • Crossing 90% turns a B into an A on the unweighted scale (3.0 → 4.0) — often the highest-leverage move.
  • • Weighted GPA moves ±0.1 for every 1% change while the grade stays ≥ 70.
  • • Below 70 both GPAs receive 0 points for that course under FISD rules.
  • • Use multi-scenario to plan end-of-semester targets across several classes.
